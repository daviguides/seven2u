# Seven2U — Positioning

## Category

Interactive TV series companion — part of a streaming platform ecosystem.

## One-Liner

Personal series tracker with AI-powered insights that helps you discover, follow, and reflect on your viewing journey.

## Jobs-to-be-Done

| When... | I want to... | So that... |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| I find a new series | know if it matches my taste | I don't waste time on something I won't enjoy |
| I watch an episode | mark it and jot down thoughts | I remember my reaction and where I stopped |
| I return after weeks | see exactly where I left off | I resume without rewatching or guessing |
| I browse episodes ahead | get a spoiler-free preview | I decide what to watch tonight |

## Differentiator

AI Insights — not generic recommendations ("because you watched X"), but content analysis. What THIS series/episode IS: tone, themes, character dynamics. Personalized when user comments exist.

## Audience

Series enthusiasts who juggle multiple shows and value organized, reflective watching over passive consumption.

## Competitive Landscape

| Product | What it does | What it lacks |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| TV Time | Track watched episodes | No AI insights, no comments per episode |
| Trakt | Comprehensive tracking + stats | Complex, no AI analysis |
| Letterboxd | Film diary + reviews | Movies only, no episode-level tracking |
| IMDb | Info + ratings | No personal tracking, no AI insights |

Seven2U occupies the intersection: **tracking + AI content analysis** in a single, focused experience.

## Tone

- Confident but not loud
- Informative but not clinical
- Personal but not social
- Modern streaming platform aesthetic

## Success Metrics (for the challenge)

- Architecture & Design: clean separation, extensible, documented trade-offs (30%)
- Functionality & Stability: search works, details load, tracking persists, AI responds (30%)
- Code Quality: readable, SOLID, cohesive (20%)
- AI Integration: isolated, provider-independent, fallback strategy (10%)
- Testability: unit-testable domain logic (10%)
